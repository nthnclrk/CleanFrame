# Historical photograph credits

This fork no longer packages the original fifteen launcher photographs or their derived materials. Their Unsplash licence and photographer/page records remain in [the source register](../../docs/asset-sources.md) and [the historical gallery catalogue](../../docs/gallery-sources.json). Earlier image files remain in Git history under their original terms.

Current Art Mode photos come from the owner’s local TV and stay in private runtime caches; they are not included in this widget.
